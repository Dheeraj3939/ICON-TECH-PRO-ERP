## Welcome & Executive Overview

### What the ERP Does
The **ICON TECH PRO ERP** is a custom-built, unified enterprise management system designed specifically for a **Technology Reseller and Systems Integrator**. It seamlessly connects customer enquiries, on-site surveys, quotations, commercial discount approvals, distributor procurement, office inventory, site delivery, installation job cards, client handover, GST tax invoicing, payment tracking, and warranty/AMC contracts into one real-time Command Center.

### Why It Matters to ICON TECH PRO
Before this ERP, information was scattered across paper files, spreadsheets, personal chat messages, and separate accounting packages. Quotation margins were calculated manually, distributor purchase costs were exposed on printed sheets, and post-installation warranty expirations were forgotten. 

This ERP solves those challenges:
- **Zero Double-Entry**: An enquiry turns into a quotation in 1 click; an accepted quotation becomes a sales order in 1 click; a sales order generates the invoice in 1 click.
- **Strict Commercial Privacy**: Sales representatives can create proposals without seeing sensitive distributor purchase costs or company margins.
- **Total Visibility**: Management sees real-time receivables, pipeline values, and technician workloads at a glance.
- **Never Miss a Renewal**: Expiring warranties and AMC contracts are automatically flagged for proactive customer outreach.
