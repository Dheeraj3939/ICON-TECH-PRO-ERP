## 2. End-to-End Business Workflows Verified

### 2.1 Workflow 1: CRM Lead Intake & Controlled Customer Sourcing
- **Objective:** Ensure all inbound prospective clients are captured with accurate contact metadata, canonical sourcing attribution, and permanent salesperson assignment.
- **Canonical Sources (Exact 7):**
  1. `DIRECT_INQUIRY` (Direct Walk-in / Inbound Contact)
  2. `OFFICIAL_WEBSITE` (Website Inquiry Form / Digital Portal)
  3. `TELEPHONIC_INBOUND` (Direct Corporate Telephone Call)
  4. `EXISTING_CLIENT_REFERRAL` (Referral by Existing Active Client)
  5. `OEM_PARTNER_REFERRAL` (Referral by OEM Partner: BenQ, Epson, Samsung, etc.)
  6. `FIELD_EXECUTIVE_SOURCING` (Direct B2B Sourcing by Field Sales Executive)
  7. `GOVERNMENT_TENDER_PORTAL` (GeM / State / Central Govt Tender Portal)
- **Legacy Compatibility:** Existing records with non-canonical values are displayed gracefully in read views; edits prompt updating to canonical values.
- **Salesperson Lock:** Upon customer creation, the assigning sales executive is permanently bound. Modification requires Administrative or Managing Director role elevation.
- **Test Result:** **PASSED**. Form validation rejects arbitrary strings; customer codes (CUST-YYYY-XXXX) generate sequentially without collisions.

### 2.4 Workflow 4: CPQ Engine & Quotation Workspace Rebuild
- **Objective:** Deliver a human-friendly, responsive quotation editor supporting complex CPQ structures, automated pricing calculations, and zero-leak customer outputs.
- **Line-Item Price Modes:**
  - **Mode A: Before GST (Exclusive):** Unit price entered net of tax. CGST/SGST (intra-state) or IGST (inter-state) calculated automatically based on customer state code vs ICON TECH PRO home state (Tamil Nadu - 33).
  - **Mode B: Inclusive of GST:** Total customer-facing price entered. Net base price and tax breakdown back-calculated with zero floating-point drift:
    $$\text{Taxable Value} = \frac{\text{Inclusive Amount}}{1 + \frac{\text{GST Rate}}{100}}$$
- **Confidential Margin & Supplier Cost Redaction:**
  - Sales reps see purchase costs and margin indicators in the workspace (subject to RBAC).
  - Customer printout modal (`PrintQuotationModal.tsx`) strictly strips purchase price, supplier names, and margins.
  - Zero React Rules of Hooks errors; all modal state hooks initialize unconditionally.
- **Test Result:** **PASSED**. Calculation precision verified up to 4 decimal places, rounded to 2 for final billing.

### 2.5 Workflow 5: Quotation Revisioning & Lifecycle Control
- **Objective:** Manage formal commercial proposals through revision lifecycles with strict document locking upon client commitment.
- **Revision Progression:** `QT-2026-0001-R0` $\rightarrow$ `QT-2026-0001-R1` $\rightarrow$ `QT-2026-0001-R2`.
- **Lock Invariants:**
  - Approving a quotation locks revision fields.
  - Generating an invoice or delivery challan locks all preceding quotation items permanently.
  - Superseded revisions remain accessible for historical audit.
- **Test Result:** **PASSED**. Database constraints reject updates to locked quotation revisions.

### 2.6 Workflow 6: Customer Purchase Order (PO) Matching
- **Objective:** Validate incoming client purchase orders against accepted quotation terms before converting to an active Sales Order.
- **Validation Checks:**
  1. PO Number and PO Date mandatory.
  2. Value tolerance check: Discrepancies between PO value and Quotation value flagged for manager approval if exceeding 0.5%.
  3. Scope items matched line-by-line.
- **Test Result:** **PASSED**. Sales Order generation blocked until PO metadata is verified and uploaded.

### 2.7 Workflow 7: Proforma Invoices (Zero Accounting Impact)
- **Objective:** Issue commercial proforma invoices for advance payment requests without prematurely triggering GST liability or booking revenue in the General Ledger.
- **Functional Invariants:**
  - Sequence format: `PI/YYYY-YY/XXXX` (e.g. `PI/2026-27/0104`).
  - Proforma document carries explicit disclaimer: *"This is a Proforma Invoice for advance payment processing only. This is not a Tax Invoice under GST law."*
  - Zero stock deduction: Inventory reserves remain uncommitted until Delivery Challan or Tax Invoice creation.
  - Full conversion path to Tax Invoice once advance receipt is confirmed.
- **Test Result:** **PASSED**. Automated suite 3 verified zero accounting ledger entries on PI creation.

---

### 2.8 Workflow 8: Delivery Challans (Rule 55 CGST Act Compliant)
- **Objective:** Authorize physical movement of capital goods, demonstration units, and project equipment under statutory Indian GST rules.
- **Statutory Requirements:**
  - Rule 55 4-copy format:
    1. *Original for Consignee*
    2. *Duplicate for Transporter*
    3. *Triplicate for Dispatch & Security*
    4. *Quadruplicate for Office Records*
  - Mandatory fields: Challan Number (`DC/YYYY-YY/XXXX`), Date, Consignor & Consignee GSTIN, HSN Code, Goods Description, Quantity, Unit, Taxable Value, Tax Rates, Place of Supply, Vehicle Registration Number, Driver Contact, Transporter Name, and Purpose of Movement (`SUPPLY_ON_APPROVAL`, `JOB_WORK`, `SITE_DEMO`, `PROJECT_INSTALLATION`).
- **Test Result:** **PASSED**. Rule 55 print templates render clean multi-copy pagination with verified HSN summaries.
